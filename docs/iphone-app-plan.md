# Selfnote for iPhone

**Implementation plan · November 17, 2026**

Start a voice note from one familiar control, speak as soon as the recorder is ready, and tap **Stop & Save**. Selfnote saves the audio immediately and turns it into an editable page on the phone. Design for the iPhone 13 Pro Max first. Use the same recording action on newer iPhones so the button can change without changing how notes work.

The starting point is `casruta/selfnote` at `96c2e88` on `main`. Its README contains only “# selfnote”, alongside an MIT license. The targets, files, and milestones below are proposed additions. [Repository at the reviewed commit](https://github.com/casruta/selfnote/tree/96c2e88e689f42e0b5a039c17a6f115b727b8d0c)

## Which button starts the note?

Apple assigns the right-side triple-click on this iPhone to Accessibility Shortcut. Its guide describes the gesture as a way to “turn the accessibility features of your choice on or off.” Apple documents no public binding for an ordinary note app to take over that gesture. The 13 Pro Max also has a Ring/Silent switch where some later phones have an Action button. [Accessibility Shortcut](https://support.apple.com/en-au/guide/iphone/iph3e2e31a5/ios) · [iPhone 13 Pro Max controls](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/guide/iphone/iphb58c09dcb/ios)

| Where | How to start | Setup after installing Selfnote |
| --- | --- | --- |
| iPhone 13 Pro Max and newer iPhones with Back Tap, including models without an Action button | Triple-tap the **back** of the phone. | In Shortcuts, save a shortcut named **Record Selfnote** containing the app’s **Start Recording** action. Select it under **Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap → Triple Tap**. [Apple’s Back Tap instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-ca/guide/shortcuts/apd897693606/ios) |
| iPhone 13 Pro Max and newer supported models, visible button | Tap **Record** in the app or in Control Center. On the 13 Pro Max, also offer **Record** as a bottom Lock Screen control. | Add Selfnote from the controls gallery. For the Lock Screen, customize a bottom control on the chosen wallpaper. The app works from its own Record button immediately after microphone setup. [Apple’s controls guide](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/widgetkit/creating-controls-to-perform-actions-across-the-system) |
| iPhones with an Action button, such as the 15 Pro | Press and hold the **Action button**. | Choose **Settings → Action Button → Shortcut → Record Selfnote**. The shortcut starts the same recorder. [Action button setup](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-and-customize-the-action-button-iphe89d61d66/ios) |

**Try the gesture before building.** Assign a shortcut containing **Show Notification** to Triple Tap and try ten times on the 13 Pro Max with its usual case. Count activations. This checks whether Back Tap feels dependable in practice. The signed app will test microphone startup separately. [Apple’s Show Notification action](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/guide/shortcuts/apd2175adcab/ios)

**Optional experiment with the exact side-button gesture.** If Magnifier appears in both settings, select it as the sole Accessibility Shortcut and make a personal Shortcuts automation for **App → Magnifier → Is Opened → Show Notification**. Test whether a side-button triple-click produces the notification on this phone. The automation also fires when Magnifier opens normally, so connecting it to automatic recording would make ordinary Magnifier use start the microphone. Apple documents the two features individually. Treat their combination as a personal experiment. [Opening Magnifier](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/guide/iphone/iphe867dc99c/ios) · [App-open automations](https://support.apple.com/en-ph/guide/shortcuts/apde31e9638b/ios)

**Locked-phone behavior.** The first build should bring capture to the foreground and start automatically once iOS grants access. Honor any unlock prompt. Test a sleeping display, an awake but genuinely locked phone, and locking during a recording separately. An interactive Stop button inside a Live Activity requires authentication on a locked device. Keep Stop available in the app and limit the Lock Screen display to recording status, elapsed time, and Stop. [App Intent execution modes](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appintents/appintent/supportedmodes) · [Widget and Live Activity interactions](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/widgetkit/adding-interactivity-to-widgets-and-live-activities)

## What a note looks like

The inbox shows recent notes with a title, date, and short preview. A new recording appears there while transcription is pending. Search finds text in titles and bodies. Each note has an editable title, readable paragraphs, and tappable checklist items. **Original transcript** and **Play recording** let the owner check a name, amount, or missed word. Ordinary speech becomes prose. Optional spoken cues such as “new paragraph”, “bullet”, and “to do” give the speaker control over structure.

For example, suppose the speaker says *“Balcony garden. The basil needs afternoon shade. To do, buy two clay pots. I might plant mint later.”* The saved page should read like this.

> **Balcony garden**
>
> The basil needs afternoon shade.
>
> ☐ Buy two clay pots.
>
> I might plant mint later.

Use the first short phrase for a suggested title, preserve sentence order, and keep qualifiers such as “might” and “don’t”. Generate a checkbox only from an explicit task cue in a language with tested formatting rules. Keep the original recognition result alongside the editable page. **Share Markdown** gives the owner a portable copy with headings, paragraphs, and checkboxes.

## Language and platform

Use **Swift and SwiftUI**. The parts that decide whether this app feels good are microphone startup, Shortcuts, Lock Screen controls, and offline speech recognition. Apple exposes all four through native frameworks. Swift lets the recorder, intent, and UI share one implementation. SwiftData can store the notes locally, and Markdown is enough for the editable body. A second app runtime would add a native bridge to the most important code. [App Intents](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appintents) · [Apple Speech](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/speech) · [SwiftData](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftdata/modelcontainer)

**Target iOS 26 or later.** The 13 Pro Max can run it, and it brings both speech transcribers under one API. The owner’s installed iOS version and preferred dictation language still need confirmation. If the phone must stay on iOS 18, add the older foreground intent setting (`openAppWhenRun`) and a separate `SFSpeechURLRecognitionRequest` path with speech authorization. Check `supportsOnDeviceRecognition` and set `requiresOnDeviceRecognition` before submitting audio. Test the legacy recognizer’s duration limits separately. [Compatible iPhones](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iphone-models-compatible-with-ios-26-iphe3fa5df43/26) · [SpeechTranscriber availability](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/speech/speechtranscriber.md) · [Legacy recognizer](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/speech/sfspeechrecognizer) · [On-device recognition requirement](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/speech/sfspeechrecognitionrequest/requiresondevicerecognition)

## How to write the first version

Start with one Xcode project, an iOS app target, and one WidgetKit extension for the control and recording Live Activity. Build and sign on a Mac with Xcode and an SDK that supports the test phone. Pair the 13 Pro Max and enable Developer Mode for local testing. Use a second physical iPhone with an Action button to qualify that route. [Running on a device](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/enabling-developer-mode-on-a-device)

| Proposed code location | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| `Selfnote/Capture/` | A single serialized `CaptureController` owns the audio session, file, current note ID, start, stop, and interruptions. UI and system actions call the same operations. |
| `Selfnote/Intents/` and `SelfnoteWidgets/` | Publish Start Recording as an App Shortcut. The extension draws the control and Live Activity. Route Start into the foreground app and send Stop for the active note ID to the app process using a Live Activity intent. One app-owned recorder handles both. [LiveActivityIntent](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appintents/liveactivityintent) |
| `Selfnote/Transcription/` | Select the local model and language, prepare assets, and transcribe a finished audio file. Treat a note’s saved processing state as the work queue. |
| `Selfnote/Notes/` | SwiftData model, pure transcript-to-Markdown formatter, inbox, and editor. Title and body are editable. Preview renders paragraphs, bullets, and checkboxes from the same stored body. |
| `SelfnoteTests/` and `SelfnoteUITests/` | Test formatting and state transitions with fixed inputs. Exercise the inbox and editor in the simulator. Keep button, microphone, lock, and language-model qualification on physical phones. |

1. **Start capture deliberately.** During first-use setup, ask for microphone access with `AVAudioApplication.requestRecordPermission()` and `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription`. Persist one note ID and allocate its recording file. Activate `AVAudioSession` and start `AVAudioRecorder`. Show the timer and give a brief haptic cue once the recorder starts. Enable recording-session haptics and verify the cue on the phone. Repeating Start while active reports the existing session. [Microphone permission](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avfaudio/avaudioapplication/requestrecordpermission%28completionhandler%3A%29) · [Audio recorder](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avfaudio/avaudiorecorder) · [Haptics during recording](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avfaudio/avaudiosession/allowhapticsandsystemsoundsduringrecording)
2. **Make recording visible and stoppable.** Use `AudioRecordingIntent` for the system recording actions. Enable Live Activities and keep the recording activity active until Stop. Apple warns, “If you don’t start a Live Activity, the audio recording stops.” If activities are disabled or startup fails, direct the user to the explicit in-app Record button. Enable the audio background mode for an ongoing recording. On an interruption, close the playable portion and let the owner choose when to record again. [AudioRecordingIntent](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appintents/audiorecordingintent) · [Audio session background behavior](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avfaudio/avaudiosession/category-swift.struct/record)
3. **Save before processing.** Keep an app-private audio file named for the note ID. On Stop, close the file, end the activity, and persist the pending transcription. A second Stop for the same ID does nothing. On relaunch or unlock, reconcile the file and saved state. Queue finalized, readable clips and mark unreadable partial clips as interrupted. Use iOS file protection compatible with writing an open recording across lock. Resume reading protected audio after unlock. [File protection](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundation/fileprotectiontype/completeunlessopen)
4. **Transcribe locally.** Check `SpeechTranscriber.isAvailable` and support for the chosen locale. Use `DictationTranscriber` when it supports the device and language that the preferred model cannot serve. Prepare language assets through `AssetInventory` during setup. After Stop, pass the saved file to `SpeechAnalyzer` and persist final segments in order. A missing download shows **Waiting for language download**. For an unsupported language, keep the audio playable and offer language selection or manual editing. Apple says these modules “don’t send audio data of the user’s voice to Apple’s servers.” Its separate speech-authorization flow applies to the legacy recognizer. [SpeechTranscriber](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/speech/speechtranscriber) · [DictationTranscriber](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/speech/dictationtranscriber) · [Speech assets](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/speech/assetinventory) · [Speech privacy](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/speech/asking-permission-to-use-speech-recognition)
5. **Commit one editable note.** Store `id`, creation time, language, audio filename, processing state, original transcript, title, Markdown body, and manual edit time. The normal states are `recording → queued → transcribing → ready`. Requeue a stale transcription at the next foreground opportunity or unlock. Save the transcript and first draft together. If the owner edited while processing, add the transcript and keep their edits. Retry uses the existing ID. Keep audio for replay until the owner removes it or deletes the note. Deleting a note cancels pending work and removes its audio. Use local storage and normal device backup settings.

## Build order and acceptance tests

| Milestone and owner | Deliverable | Pass condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Capture engineer | A signed build with Record, Back Tap setup, the system control, Live Activity, Stop, and audio replay. | On the unlocked phone, aim for 10/10 playable clips from the in-app and Control Center buttons, and at least 9/10 from Back Tap in the usual case. On the second phone also require 10/10 Action-button starts. Listen for the first word after the cue and the last word before Stop. Require zero duplicate clips. |
| 2 · Speech engineer | Automatic transcription on Stop, language setup, and a retry using the saved file. | With the language installed, a 30-second note produces readable text in airplane mode on both devices. Before a download or with an unsupported locale, the audio survives. Close the app after Stop, reopen it, and get one note under the original ID. |
| 3 · Notes engineer | Inbox, editable page, simple formatter, search, playback, delete, and Markdown sharing. | Search for a spoken phrase, edit the title, check a task, and reopen the app. The edits appear in shared Markdown. Interrupt transcription, retry, and verify manual edits persist. Delete during processing and verify the note stays deleted. Test “don’t”, “might”, a name, and an amount against the original recognition result. |
| 4 · Independent device tester | A compatibility table recording model, OS, language, trigger, phone state, outcome, and any unlock prompt. | Test foreground, another app in use, cold launch, genuinely locked with Face ID covered, display asleep, and lock during recording. Also test denied mic access, disabled Live Activities, a call, unavailable storage, and rapid repeated Start/Stop. Confirm an honest status and recovery in each case. Check Record and Stop with VoiceOver and large text. |

The first milestone decides which routes go into onboarding. Aim for a ready cue within two seconds on at least nine of ten warm, unlocked trials. Report cold starts and time spent unlocking separately. Advertise locked-start behavior only for phone states that passed. The speech and notes engineers can use the same sample recording and expected page while the capture engineer tests hardware. One integrator owns the Xcode project and signing settings.

**Keep the first release focused.** Deliver capture, transcription, basic page formatting, editing, search, and ownership of the recorded material. Defer cloud sync, accounts, collaboration, direct Notion integration, and AI rewriting. Markdown export provides an immediate route out of the app. A later opt-in rewrite can present a draft for review while preserving the original. The 13 Pro Max can use the core design independently of Apple Intelligence eligibility. [Apple Intelligence device requirements](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121115)
